Section 4

Applicable Legal Framework

Subject to the facts, evidence, ownership status, registrations, intent, and other requirements of each case, conduct described above may engage remedies or offences under Indian law, including:

  • Copyright Act, 1957: Section 51 addresses infringement; Section 55 provides civil remedies such as injunctions and damages; Section 57 protects an author's right to claim authorship and certain rights against prejudicial distortion.
  • Trade Marks Act, 1999: Section 27(2) preserves passing-off remedies, and Section 29 addresses infringement of a registered trade mark where its statutory requirements are met.
  • Information Technology Act, 2000: Sections 66C and 66D may apply to electronic identity theft or cheating by personation where their legal elements are satisfied.
  • Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023: Section 356 addresses defamation, subject to its statutory requirements and exceptions. A false statement is not automatically defamation merely because ExamReady disputes it.

These references are general notice only and are not a legal opinion. The appropriate claim, remedy, forum, and limitation period depend on the particular conduct and should be assessed by a qualified lawyer.

Official text: Copyright Act, Trade Marks Act, Information Technology Act, and Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita.
